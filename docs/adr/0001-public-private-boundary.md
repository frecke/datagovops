# ADR 0001: Separate public model from private method

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-13

## Context

The work must support academic traceability, public thought leadership and reusable commercial delivery without exposing customer-confidential material or giving away every implementation method.

## Decision

Publish conceptual models, open profiles, schemas, fictional examples and selected reference implementations. Keep detailed assessment methods, scoring, customer mappings, workshop playbooks and accelerators private.

## Consequences

Public artifacts must stand on their own and cite sources. Private artifacts may reference public identifiers but must not be required to understand the public model. Promotion from private to public requires confidentiality, licensing and research-integrity review.
