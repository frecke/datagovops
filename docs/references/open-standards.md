## Profiling questions

- Is the proposed field already represented by the parent standard?
- Is an extension, controlled vocabulary, external reference or implementation rule the least disruptive solution?
- Does the extension preserve valid parent-standard documents?
- Is the requirement Swedish, organization-specific or broadly reusable?
- Does the artifact map to catalog metadata without pretending the two models are identical?
