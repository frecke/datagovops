- Peffers, K., Tuunanen, T., Rothenberger, M. A., and Chatterjee, S. (2007).
  *A Design Science Research Methodology for Information Systems Research*.
  [DOI: 10.2753/MIS0742-1222240302](https://doi.org/10.2753/MIS0742-1222240302).
- Hevner, A. R., March, S. T., Park, J., and Ram, S. (2004).
  *Design Science in Information Systems Research*.
  [DOI: 10.2307/25148625](https://doi.org/10.2307/25148625).

## Intended use

These sources support the overall Design Science Research framing. They do not predetermine:

- the artifact boundary;
- the case-selection strategy;
- expert-review protocol;
- evaluation criteria;
- evidence strength;
- the claims that the resulting study may defensibly make.

Those decisions require a study-specific research design and supervisor review.
