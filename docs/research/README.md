# Research inception

This directory contains the public, non-case-specific research foundation for the DataGovOps study.

> Status: pre-thesis exploratory work. The thesis topic, research design and formal empirical study have not yet been approved by a university supervisor.

## Public scope

The public repository may contain:

- reproducible literature-search protocols and search logs;
- bibliographic metadata and non-infringing annotations;
- authoritative public-source reviews;
- provisional problem framing, purpose and research questions;
- provisional Design Science Research (DSR) design;
- public artifact definitions, synthetic examples and evaluation criteria.

## Private boundary

The following must remain in the private repository or an approved university research environment:

- named or identifiable case-organization information;
- interviews, transcripts, consent records and personal data;
- unpublished expert-review responses and raw evaluation data;
- customer-specific requirements and mappings;
- Redpill Linpro assessment models, scoring methods, delivery playbooks and accelerators.

## Research integrity

Pre-thesis work is exploratory. Formal data collection and summative evaluation must not begin until course enrolment, supervisor approval, organizational permission and any applicable ethics or data-protection steps are resolved.

Claims in this directory use these labels:

- **Verified fact:** supported by an inspected source.
- **Working interpretation:** reasoned from sources but not itself stated by them.
- **Design proposal:** a candidate feature or structure for the artifact.
- **Research hypothesis:** a proposition that requires evaluation.
- **Open question:** unresolved and not safe to present as a conclusion.
